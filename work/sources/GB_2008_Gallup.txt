"PRINCETON, NJ -- Gallup's final pre-election allocated estimate of
the national 2008 vote for Congress -- from Gallup Poll Daily
tracking conducted Oct. 31-Nov. 2 -- gives the Democrats a 12
percentage-point lead over the Republicans among likely voters, 53%
to 41%."

"The final estimate is based on Gallup's traditional likely voter
model, and assumes a turnout rate of 64% of the voting-age
population. ... the Democrats' advantage among traditional likely
voters is not much different from their standing among all registered
voters, among whom they hold a 15-point lead."

Survey Methods: "Results are based on telephone interviews with
3,050 national adults, aged 18 and older, conducted Oct. 31-Nov. 2,
2008 as part of Gallup Poll Daily tracking. ... Results for likely
voters (based on Gallup's 'traditional' likely voter model ...) are
based on the subsample of 2,472 survey respondents deemed most likely
to vote in the November 2008 general election..."
